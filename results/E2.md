# E2: F1 baselines on development folds: Demo data (synthetic)

Task 7.3 (file 11). **Demo data (synthetic).** Pre-freeze development-fold numbers (file 11 §8 risk 4): regenerated after the `ps_v1` tag (G2) with `make results` (= `python -m scripts.score_e2`). No claim about real-market accuracy.

Spec seed 20261124; SYN and SYN-NULL generated in memory; development period to 2025-12-18 (the holdout year is cut before QC, features and folds). Folds (params/ps_v1.yaml `f1_eval`, D-033): 6 expanding-window folds from 2024-06, test 3 months, step 3, embargo = h days. MASE = MAE of q50 / MAE of B-N on the same test rows (D-016), 95% circular block-bootstrap CI over days (block 10, 2000 resamples, seed 0).

Baselines (file 04 §1.2): **B-N** q50 = M(t-1). **B-SN** q50 = M(t+h-8), the target's weekday one week earlier as known at t (newest earlier known quote if missing, else M(t-1)); **at h = 7 that day is t-1, so B-SN equals B-N by definition (MASE exactly 1), not a finding.** **B-SR** q50 = M(t-1) x exp(-spread_region/2), halfway in logs to the crop's median over all mandis, same for every h. Ranges: training-row quantiles of log(y/q50).

Full table (variant x crop x h x baseline, all file 04 §1.4 metrics): `results/E2.csv`.

## Reading the numbers

- B-N is the bar: neither other baseline beats it on any crop or horizon, on SYN or SYN-NULL. E3 (11.1) must beat B-N, not these.
- B-SN loses most at h = 1 (it uses a quote up to a week older than M(t-1)) and converges to B-N as h -> 7.
- B-SR pulls every mandi halfway to the crop median, but the generator gives each mandi a fixed premium (spec `mandi_effect`), so the median is not where prices go back to. On SYN-NULL each mandi also has its own random walk (D-014), so spreads drift apart over the years and halving them costs most (MASE about 6-16).
- B-SR ranges come from training residuals; on SYN-NULL the spreads keep growing after training, so coverage falls to about 64%.
