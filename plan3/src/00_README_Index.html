3. Conventions

TagMeaning
VERIFIEDWe checked it ourselves (date given) or it is an official primary source
REPORTEDA secondary source, a document or a person says so; not checked by us
ASSUMPTIONChosen value we act on until evidence changes it
BELIEF – VERIFYMust be checked before anything depends on it
CORRECTEDAn earlier statement or decision replaced by a newer one (reason given)
P0 P1 P2Red-team severity: wrong number/advice or invalid claim / visible malfunction / degradation
CRIT PAR OPTExecution tags: critical path / parallel / first to drop
  • IDs: R# research item · D# decision · C# component (RFC) · R-## red-team issue · E# FYP-I result · S# success criterion · M#/G# gate · x.y task (day.number).
  • Meta facts (test-number limit, §4.7, pricing, button/list limits) were verified for Manzil on 1 Oct 2026 and carried over; re-check on Day 1 during Meta setup.
  • Data rules that never change: no scraping of Zarai Mandi; no redistribution of raw AMIS data; synthetic data is always labelled; secrets never in the repo.
