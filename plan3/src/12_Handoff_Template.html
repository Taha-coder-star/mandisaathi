Rules for writing a handoff

  • Every "done" row links to evidence a teammate can check in under 2 minutes: a commit, a passing test name, a results file, a screenshot of the WhatsApp reply, or a hash in DECISIONS.md.
  • "Done" means the task's binary DONE condition in file 11 is met. A task that is not done is reported as PARTIAL or BLOCKED with the reason; never round up.
  • Facts carry the status tags: VERIFIED (we ran or saw it), REPORTED (a document or person says so), ASSUMPTION, BELIEF – VERIFY, CORRECTED.
  • "What I need from you" is ordered, and each item says who, by when, and what unblocks.
  • Never paste secrets (tokens, keys, phone numbers) in a handoff; name the .env variable instead.
  • Data rows mention their source: SYN (synthetic, FYP-I) or the real source (FYP-II).
