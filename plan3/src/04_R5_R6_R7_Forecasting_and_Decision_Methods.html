1.2 Selected pipeline

  1. Target: y = log M(s, t+h−1) − log M(s, t−1) for series s (crop × mandi × grade), issue day t, horizon h ∈ 1…7; M = mid price (min+max)/2. The last usable quote is t−1 (rule T1).
  2. Features (FS-S): own lags and rolling stats; spread to the regional median and to the 3 nearest mandis; arrivals (if any) vs seasonal norm; weekday, day-of-year, days to Ramadan/Eid, holidays; rain in the production district (observed, and forecast as issued on t−1); crop, mandi, perishability, h.
  3. Baselines: B-N naive (price stays at M(t−1)), B-SN same weekday last week, B-SR spread reversion halfway to the regional median.
  4. Model: LightGBM, one model per quantile (0.1, 0.5, 0.9) across all series and horizons; ≈ 30 tuning trials on development folds only.
  5. Range: split-conformal correction per crop × horizon on the latest calibration fold to reach 80% coverage.
  6. Coverage gate: only series in series_coverage.eligible_f1 get a forecast; others get "andaza nahi" in the bot.
  7. Reasons: top SHAP drivers mapped to reason codes (ARRIVALS_HIGH, RAMADAN_NEAR, RAIN_RECENT, SPREAD_HIGH…) with fixed Urdu/English text.

1.4 Evaluation and pass criterion

  • Rolling origin: train before the fold, test the next 3 months, roll by 3 months; ≥ 6 development folds.
  • Metrics: MAE (PKR/100 kg), MASE vs B-N, pinball loss, coverage and width of the 80% range, direction accuracy for moves > 5%.
  • S1 (lockbox): pooled MASE < 1 at h = 1–3 with the 95% block-bootstrap upper bound < 1; coverage 75–85%. Valid null: "short-term prices behaved like a random walk in the test year".

Verdict: B as the main model, A as the bar, C only if it beats B in development folds. D is FYP-II optional, only if area-sown data is released before issue dates (R6 open item).

2.2 Selected pipeline

  1. Target: z = log R(c, r, M) − log Clim(c, r, month(M)); R = monthly average real price (CPI-deflated), issued at M−k, k ∈ {3, 4, 5}.
  2. Features (FS-L): z same month last year; mean z over last harvest season; latest z, 3-month trend, 6-month volatility; sowing-window rain/temperature anomalies as observed by issue time; optional area anomaly.
  3. Output: P10/P50/P90 in nominal rupees (re-inflated at the latest CPI) + probabilities of below / near / above normal (tercile of past z).
  4. Honesty rule: where development skill ≤ 0 for a crop, the bot ships climatology ("is mahine ka aam rate") and says the outlook adds nothing for that crop.

2.3 Evaluation and pass criterion

  • Leave-future-out from 2015: train on years before Y, issue outlooks for Y; lockbox year sealed. Power analysis rerun for the monthly design at G1.
  • Metrics: CRPS and pinball vs climatology, tercile hit rate, Brier skill, 80% coverage.
  • S3: pooled CRPS skill > 0 with the bootstrap CI lower bound > 0. Valid null: "harvest-month prices were not predictable beyond seasonal averages 3–5 months ahead".

Verdict: C, fully deterministic, no LLM inside.

3.2 Selected rule

Net(m) = Q × P̂(m, t+ℓm) × (1 − cm) − T(dm, Q) − Fm − Q × P̂ × δcrop × ℓm
  • Q quantity (100 kg units); P̂ the F1 P50 at arrival day; ℓm travel delay (0 home mandi; 1 day if road distance > 80 km ASSUMPTION); cm commission (default 6% ASSUMPTION); T = road_km × cost per km for the vehicle size implied by Q (from the diesel price); Fm market/unloading fees; δ spoilage per day (tomato 4%, onion/potato 1%, wheat 0% ASSUMPTION).
  • Candidates: eligible mandis within 150 km road distance ASSUMPTION (F2-radius in ps_v1).
  • Risk rule: a mandi other than the nearest is recommended only if its net gain over the nearest is still > 0 when computed with its P10 and the nearest's P50. Otherwise the answer is "Nazdeeki mandi theek hai" with the numbers.
  • Output: top 3 with net rupees, the gain over the nearest, the cost assumptions used (so the farmer can say "mera transport mehnga hai").
  • All cost parameters are R7 open items: confirm with ≥ 3 traders or the co-supervisor; sensitivity analysis (±50% transport, commission 4–8%, spoilage ×0.5–×2) reported with every F2 result.

3.3 Evaluation and pass criterion

  • Backtest on history: for each origin district × crop × day, compare the realised net price at the recommended mandi (PROP) with NEAREST and YESTERDAY-BEST (the mandi with the highest price yesterday).
  • S2: mean realised gain of PROP over NEAREST > 0 PKR/100 kg with the 95% CI lower bound > 0. Valid null: "after costs, the nearest mandi was as good as our advice".

4. Decisions

D6: F1 → DECIDED. Global LightGBM quantile model with split-conformal ranges, over three baselines (naive, seasonal naive, spread reversion) and SARIMA on the longest series; rolling-origin evaluation; coverage gate; SHAP reason codes. Rules T1–T8 with tests L1–L6.

D7: F3 → DECIDED. Linear quantile regression on deviations from climatology (small LightGBM only if it wins in development); tercile probabilities; climatology shipped where there is no skill.

D8: F2 → DECIDED. Deterministic net-revenue ranking over F1 P50 with a P10 risk rule and a 150 km candidate radius; cost parameters are assumptions until R7 interviews, always shown with a sensitivity analysis.
