1. FYP-I objective

Show the supervisor and the jury, on real WhatsApp (team phones plus one jury phone, our test number), that MandiSaathi:

  1. answers farmer price questions in Roman Urdu, Urdu, English, mixed text and Urdu voice notes, with only numbers that come from our data and models;
  2. tells a farmer where to sell after transport, commission and spoilage, and says "nearest mandi is fine" when the gain is not certain;
  3. keeps a price alert the farmer asked for and delivers it once, without spam;
  4. is honest about what it cannot answer;
  5. stands on first results (E1–E6) produced under frozen, pre-registered rules on synthetic data the team generated. These prove the pipeline (it recovers a planted signal and reports no skill where none exists), not real-market accuracy, which is measured on the real data in FYP-II.
Pass condition for FYP-I as a whole: all 5 scenarios (§5) pass 3 consecutive rehearsals on Android + iPhone, wording paraphrased (not scripted), with zero numbers not present in a tool result; E1–E6 are reproducible from the tagged commit with make results; G2 freeze artefacts exist (ps_v1 tag, lockbox-rehearsal hashes, frozen benchmark hash).

4. Demo data requirements

  • S-DEMO snapshot of the synthetic dataset after the generated load of Fri 11 Dec 2026, forecast run R-20261211, tagged and restorable in < 2 min (file 03 §7).
  • Coverage: onion, tomato, potato, each with ≥ 5 eligible Punjab mandis including Lahore, Okara and Sahiwal; distances cached for origins Okara, Sahiwal, Kasur, Lahore; ps_v1 cost parameters.
  • Golden answers (demo/golden/*.json) computed offline by scripts/expected_answers.py from S-DEMO: the latest rate, F1 range and F2 ranking (or the "nearest is fine" outcome) for each scenario. Expected values are therefore whatever the frozen engine produces; success is judged on exact equality of facts, not on any hoped-for number.
  • Every rate shown carries the source name "Demo data (synthetic)".
  • One prepared "next-day" price row for scenario 4, loaded through the normal batch path by demo_trigger_alert.sh.
  • 5 allowlisted phones: Taha, Ashna, Umar, supervisor, one jury member (registered the day before).
  • Benchmark set (file 05 §4) as the test bed: 40 text, 30 voice + 5 controls, 30 multi-turn/commands.

Scenario 2: Where to sell (Urdu voice note ~8 s, Ashna's phone)

Start♪ "Mere paas pachaas mann pyaz hai, Okara mein. Kahan bechoon?"

3a"Aur tamatar?" → crop switch; origin and quantity kept → F2 for tomato → equals golden/s3a.json
